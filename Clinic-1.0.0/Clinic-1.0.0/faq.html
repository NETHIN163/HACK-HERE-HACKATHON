How can I book a ride in advance?

You can book a taxi through our online booking page or call our helpline. We recommend pre-booking at least 2 hours in advance for airport transfers.

02

What is your cancellation policy?

Ride cancellations are free up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup. Cancellations made within 2 hours may incur a small dispatcher fee.

03

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, UPI payments, popular mobile wallets, and cash directly to your chauffeur.

04

Do you charge extra if my flight is delayed?

No, we do not charge for flight delays. We track your flight status in real time and automatically adjust your pickup time.

05

Can I request a child safety seat?

Yes, child seats are available upon request. Please specify your need for a child safety seat in the booking form special notes.
